Acme Registry pricing and plans

Acme Registry is a universal artifact repository for every team, from a single developer to the enterprise. Pick the plan that fits how your organization builds and ships software.

Free

The Free plan costs $0 per month and includes 2 GB of storage, public repositories and community support.

Pro

The Pro plan costs $180 per month and includes 50 GB of storage, private repositories and email support.

Enterprise

The Enterprise plan is priced on request: contact sales for unlimited storage, SSO and a dedicated success manager.

Deployment

Every plan runs as a managed cloud service. Enterprise customers can also self-host Acme Registry in their own data center, including fully air-gapped installations.
